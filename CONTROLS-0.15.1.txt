CONTROLS UPDATE 0.15.1

Desktop: left-click selects; Shift+left-click adds to selection. Left-click on empty ground clears selection without issuing a move order. Right-click moves selected units, attacks a visible clicked enemy, or sets the rally point of a selected building. The explicit Attack-move action still uses a left-click destination.

Touch: tap a friendly unit to select; tap the destination or enemy to issue an order. Building placement keeps its separate confirmation controls.

Close the game before replacing files. This small update requires the complete 0.15 installation.

WINDOWS: extract SpaceInvaders-Controls-Windows-0.15.1.zip into the folder containing SpaceInvaders.exe; replace the matching files under resources/app/web.
GITHUB: extract SpaceInvaders-Controls-GitHub-0.15.1.zip at the root of the source repository; commit and push.
WEB/CPANEL: extract SpaceInvaders-Controls-Web-0.15.1.zip directly into the game website directory and replace matching files.
